about=Select an AOI polygon layer and an image (local raster, XYZ or WMS basemap). Local rasters are cut straight from the source on their own pixel grid (bit-identical pixels). XYZ layers are downloaded tile by tile in parallel - only the tiles that touch the AOI - and written on the tile grid with no resampling. The result is clipped to the AOI shape in a single multi-threaded warp (optionally reprojected to EPSG:4326), split into tiles in parallel and indexed in a GeoPackage tile index with relative paths and one-click Load/Remove Image actions. Make sure the imagery provider's terms allow offline copies.
